## Idea validation checklist
- [ ] Talk to potential users (record consent and anonymize notes).
- [ ] Define a specific customer and problem.
- [ ] Find existing competitors/substitutes.
- [ ] Estimate costs and willingness to pay using evidence.
- [ ] Build a tiny prototype or test.
- [ ] Measure results; decide continue/pivot/stop.
- [ ] Check safety, legal, environmental, and ethical considerations.
