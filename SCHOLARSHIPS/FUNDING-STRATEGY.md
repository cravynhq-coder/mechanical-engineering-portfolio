# Fully Funded Master's Preparation
**Goal:** pursue fully funded Master's programs; Germany is the first preference, with other countries/programs considered. Funding and eligibility must be verified from official sources for each intake.

## Build the profile over undergraduate years
- [ ] Maintain strong grades; understand IUBAT grading/transcript requirements.
- [ ] Build 2–4 meaningful engineering projects with documentation and validation.
- [ ] Develop relationships with faculty through consistent, substantive work.
- [ ] Prepare a strong CV and statement of purpose based on real achievements.
- [ ] Research language requirements early (IELTS/TOEFL or program-specific language).
- [ ] Learn whether each program requires GRE, publications, work experience, or specific prerequisites.
- [ ] Track official scholarship/program pages and deadlines.
- [ ] Plan document, test, application, translation, courier, visa, and travel costs separately.

## Verification checklist (before trusting any opportunity)
- [ ] Official government/university/scholarship page
- [ ] Coverage is clear (tuition, stipend, insurance, travel, etc.)
- [ ] Bangladesh eligibility confirmed
- [ ] Mechanical/automotive field and degree eligibility confirmed
- [ ] Required GPA, language, tests, experience confirmed
- [ ] Deadline and application procedure confirmed
- [ ] No suspicious fees or agents

Do not assume any scholarship is guaranteed or fully funded until its current official call confirms it.
